Findings and follow-up, in priority order:

1. **P2 — Forest-floor evaluation adds a quadratic rebuild path.** `web/src/render/ground.ts:120–123` supplies every forest center to every ground-color sample. `web/src/render/environment.ts:646–654` computes a distance to every center, including distant ones that cannot contribute. `web/src/render/world.ts:392–402` rebuilds the entire ground on reconciliation. With N tiles and F forest tiles this adds O(N×F) work, becoming quadratic at a fixed forest fraction. The render benchmark starts sampling after autoplay (`tools/environment-benchmark.mjs:54–69`), so steady frame timings alone cannot clear placement/rebuild latency. No measured slowdown is asserted. Bound the lookup to nearby forest centers (reach plus warp is only 3.8 world units), or explicitly measure and accept reconciliation latency at the supported board sizes.

2. **P3 — Runtime geometry-buffer accounting omits index buffers.** `tools/environment-verify.mjs:88–99` sums only four vertex attributes into `geometryBufferBytes`; indexed geometry is produced by `web/src/render/builder.ts:165–170`. The capture tool includes indices (`tools/environment-capture.mjs:157–158`), so identically named metrics are not comparable. Include `g.index.array.byteLength` in the audit's byte total, and preferably its fingerprint too. This is a diagnostic undercount, not a demonstrated allocation leak or a GPU-memory measurement.

Substantive limits on stronger claims:

- **Cross-tile clearance is unproven.** `web/src/render/tiles.ts:961–965` allows crowns across any matched, level/lower nature edge. The obstacle checks at lines 978–995 use only the current tile's walks, homes, entrances and water. A neighbor's nature-facing edge does not establish that its entire interior is clear. The real-board test likewise checks only each plant's owning tile (`web/tests/vegetation.test.ts:383–393`). A world-space neighboring-obstacle check is needed before claiming universal building/path clearance. No concrete intersecting board was reproduced in this restricted review; treat this as an uncovered risk, not a confirmed collision.
- **Banks and forest floors do not sample one identical final field.** `material.ts:91–100` computes meadow/lush/dry for green soil; `environment.ts:664` additionally mixes forest-floor color for terrain. World-space moisture now agrees, but banks/contact patches can still differ beside forested ground. Inspect that transition in refreshed close-ups; do not describe full terrain/bank color agreement as proven.
- **The 25% change bounds a spacing heuristic, not actual crown overlap.** `vegetation.ts:214–215` scales the root-separation threshold by up to 25%, using random size values rather than family geometry radii. It is deterministic and restricted by the core field, but does not establish a 25% maximum overlap of rendered crowns.
- **Water seam coverage is useful but narrower than full river correctness.** `water.test.ts` compares equal-height matched endpoints, including nested ribbon positions. It does not establish cascade appearance, pond junction appearance, or absence of depth artifacts. Those remain visual checks.
