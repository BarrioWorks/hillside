# Barrio Builder

Browser-first relaxing neighborhood-building game for desktop players, with basic touch support. Players construct a miniature Medellín-inspired hillside through direct placement, paths, stairs, and small environmental details. There are no accounts, ads, online inference, or economic pressure in the first playable release.

The supplied master brief is the product authority. Success means a player can immediately build, undo, save, and reload a coherent hillside neighborhood. Rust/WASM owns deterministic game rules. Three.js renders an orthographic miniature. TypeScript provides pointer input and a compact floating interface.

The brief explicitly authorizes implementation without a design checkpoint. Existing organizational identity is recorded in BRAND.md. The game's building palette includes warm architectural plaster and terracotta as specified by the game brief. UI surfaces retain the cool Barrio brand neutrals.
